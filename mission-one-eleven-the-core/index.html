About the book

Our Vision

It should be a birthright for every person to have a chance to respond to the Gospel. According to God's Word, this Gospel will be preached as a testimony to all nations and then the end will come (Matt.24:14). However, it is also clear in Scripture that Jesus will be return is imminenting soon even if the "day or hour' is not known. . These two truths should compel us greatly to take action and to do our part in finishing fulfilling the Great Commission, that every tribe, tongue, and nation will be represented in the blood washed church of Jesus Christ (Rev. 5:9) Mission One Eleven's purpose is to raise up a generation of laborers committed to reaching the unreached-people who have not had a chance, or at least an adequate chance, to respond to the gospel. There are roughly 2.8 billion people who have still not had a chance to respond to the gospel.

Who We Are

Mission One Eleven is a turnkey missions program that will excite and energize your congregation for missions work. The structure is already established, set up, and easy to implement in you're own church or school. It gives the local body of Christ full participation in completing the Great Commission of reaching "every tribe and nation" with the gospel of Jesus Christ. Mission One Eleven strengthens and complements your existing missions program by empowering you to send your own people to reach the unreached tribes throughout the world. In return, this experience greatly raises the passion and call towards mission work throughout your congregation.

The Solution

We have developed a very effective 12-week program that consists of six weeks of training and six weeks among an unreached tribe. Our goal is to present this program to the local church body to enable them to personally reach the unreached with the gospel of Jesus Christ.

  1. We would like to partner with you and your congregation to raise up laborers to take part in and run the program.
  2. We are not selling our program but rather providing the structure and initial resources required to run your own missions program. Upon partnering, all the resources necessary to help you begin training your own congregation and providing for them an opportunity to reach the unreached are yours to implement along with our help.

The goal is that people from your own congregation would rise up to carry on the program within your church or school by training the next set of students and sending them out. Also, we believe that from this opportunity, some will return with clarity of a full-time call and a path should be provided for them to pursue full-time mission work.

What the book is about

Mission One Eleven: The Core is the teaching text behind Mission One Eleven, the program Karl Hargestam founded to train believers in the local church and send them to unreached people groups. After a foreword by the program’s executive director and the vision statement, “One Chance for Every Person,” the book is laid out as a course of short lessons. Each lesson is a transcribed class followed by review questions for discussion.

The first course, The Assignment, works through People Who Change History, Open Heaven and Jesus Satisfied. It uses the return from Babylon in Ezra, and the picture of Jesus seeing the fruit of His labor in Isaiah 53, to show that every believer is qualified for God’s assignment. The second course, Unreached, moves through three lessons that end with He Will Lead: who the unreached are, the simple outline of the Gospel every outreach must carry, and how God leads people all the way to the field.

The third course, Chronological Storytelling, explains why oral cultures are best reached by telling the Bible as one story with a beginning and an end, and how to tell it across cultures. The book closes with the set of Bible stories used in the field, from creation through the life, death and resurrection of Jesus, ending with “Your Decision.”

MOE Core Teaching

By Karl Hargestam, MOE Founder

The Assignment: People Who Change History – Lesson 1

Welcome to the class, The Assignment. We are first today going to look at people who change history. We believe—I believe—that God has an assignment for every person as they reconcile with Jesus. The second most asked question is, “What is God’s purpose, or His assignment, for my life?

We’re going to explore this and today, first, I want to try to prove to you that connecting with your assignment is going to change not just your life, but the lives of everyone around you. That will be true for any church, any movement—but also for you as an individual.

So, as we look at the assignment, today, I want to try to prove first in this class that the people who connect with their assignment become the people who change history. So in this first class we’re going to look at three things that we think many times, I believe, are standing in the way of connecting with your assignment and causes you not to connect with it. How are you going to find it and become a history maker? So the people that change history we believe—I believe—connect with their assignment. In this first segment I want to explore the number one thing. I’m going to look at the story from the book of Ezra. Throughout God’s scripture, you have this amazing truth that God’s people, in both the Old and New Testament, journeyed and God saved them. He had to plan, like the people of Israel in captivity, that were saved from slavery but really also with an assignment to take the promised land. Or, you know, God had an assignment to do something: conquer a land or rebuild a temple. So I’m going to look at today, first, the story that’s found in the book of Ezra, the book of Nehemiah, and the book of Haggai. God’s people were in captivity, they were in Babylon, and God saves them from this, but he also gives them an assignment. This assignment is found in the first chapter of the book of Ezra, Ezra 1:3. They are in captivity and the King, Cyrus the Great, speaks to them and he says, “I have an assignment for you. I want you to go back to Jerusalem and rebuild the house of the Lord.” This became the assignment. In the new covenant, in New Testament context, God’s assignment for the people of God is to build a new house of God. That’s not out of brick and mortar of stone, but as we believe the Bible says we are living stones fitted together making the house of God. Jesus is the chief cornerstone and every tribe, language, people, and nation will be fitted in that house of God that we are to build. That is the assignment Jesus left with the people, his disciples. He said, “Go preach the gospel to every person. Make disciples of all nations.” I believe that assignment is not finished. I’m inviting you to be a part of that, and we’re going to explore what that means, how you can connect with it, and how it can change your life.

Here, now, today, first… Then the question becomes, why are people not connecting, or finding the assignment? You might even sit and look at this and say, “I want to find my assignment, I want to connect with it.” The question then becomes, why is God not interested in you finding your assignment? I believe God wants you to find your assignment even more than you, but how do you journey or discover it? One of the things that keeps us from finding it is that we don’t feel qualified. We think, “Man, I don’t have any gifts, I feel this dream God has in my life, but maybe I can’t connect with it. I don’t have the talents or I am not whole enough.” There may be many reasons why we don’t connect.

In the story of Ezra when God speaks to the people, what is interesting is that here are God’s people—they are in Babylon, they’re looking at captivity and here comes good news. God speaks to them and says, “Anyone who considers himself to be belonging to God may go out to Jerusalem and build a house of the Lord.” Think about it. The assignment was to build a temple of Solomon; it is not like God was picky about the building project. He didn’t give measurements, or what materials to use; He didn’t instruct that the temple had to be built in a specific way. He didn’t say, “I want masons, I want special builders. I want the ones that have the right skills, they’re the only ones qualified.” In fact, God was saying that the qualification was that if you consider yourself belonging to God, you can go to Jerusalem. The point is, if you are washed in the blood of Jesus, if you are saved, you are qualified for your assignment. That is the basis for qualification. Today I want you to look at this. So many times we are measuring, feeling, deciding whether we can respond to God’s assignment based on thinking, “Am I somehow whole enough? Do I have the gifts I need to fulfill God’s assignment?” The qualification, the basis that God looks to you for, is, “Are you a child of the King? Are you a child of God?” That’s what makes you qualified. In our journey trying to explore what God’s assignment is, a big challenge for many is going to unreached tribes and unreached people and looking at the tribes that have not heard the gospel, that are not connected with God’s plan for their lives. Every time we come to an unreached place or an unreached tribe we find this. It is not that they don’t want to hear the Gospel or respond when someone comes, but it’s always been, somehow, this idea that no one has come, so God’s assignment that has been given has been not completed because the people have somehow have not responded to it. So when we talk about God’s assignment, I believe it is like the following story.

I lived in Africa for a time, and if you have been to a car repair shop in Africa, you know that they can sometimes be a mess. You are lucky if the car is running when you get it back. So I had a clutch and the car was not running right, so I brought it into the shop and I just wanted a new clutch. Most of the time they can’t even find spare parts for it, but when I return a week later to pick up the car, the garage owner meets me at the door and he’s very excited and says, “Oh, car! We have done something great.” I enter the shop to take a look and they have changed a dent that was on the side. The owner continues showing me other things he has done, like he changed a light and there was a fender that he fixed.

He listed other things he had done for me and I ask, “Okay great, can I take the car? Is it fixed?”

“Oh no!” he says. “We haven’t changed the clutch yet.” So the point is, they hadn’t done their assignment! The assignment was to change the clutch. The car still couldn’t drive anywhere.

So when it comes to connecting with God’s assignment for your life, even when it comes to the church, reaching unreached people, it doesn’t matter if we do good things—the assignment is what’s important: to discover, to find, and to journey. We’re going to talk about this, how that – I believe – unlocks grace, favor and blessing over your life.”
